# Business Brief

## Situation
A consumer finance team needs to know where complaint handling is under strain: which products and issues generate the most complaints, whether responses are on time, what consumers receive, and whether anything has changed recently.

## Stakeholders and decisions

| Stakeholder | Decision | What they need |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer protection lead | Where to focus supervisory attention | Volume trends and main issues by product |
| Complaint operations manager | Where to add capacity or fix process | Timeliness by product; forwarding delays |
| Policy analyst | Whether outcomes differ by product | Monetary and non-monetary relief by product |
| Product owner, retail banking | Which products need clearer communication | Top issues and sub-issues per product |

## Business questions

| # | Question | Decision it supports | What would change the decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Which products generate the most complaints, and how has volume changed? | Where to focus | Sustained growth in one product |
| Q2 | What are the main issues within each product? | What to fix in process or communication | One issue dominating a product |
| Q3 | How do submission channels compare? | Whether channel affects handling | A channel with clearly lower timeliness |
| Q4 | How often are responses timely, and where are they late? | Which products need faster handling | A product well below the overall timely rate |
| Q5 | What outcomes do consumers get? | Whether relief differs by product | Large gaps in relief between products |
| Q6 | How do states compare per capita? | Regional focus | **Not run:** needs population data |
| Q7 | Are there spikes, and what makes them up? | Whether a spike needs investigation | A spike concentrated in one product or step |

## Scope
- **In:** complaint volume, product, issue, channel, timeliness, relief, state counts, and monthly trend, from May 2017 to August 2023.
- **Out:** company identity or company comparison (not in the data), causes of complaints, and outcomes beyond the recorded response.

## Constraints
- 2023 covers January to August only.
- Complaints from recent months are still open, so their timeliness is not yet comparable.
